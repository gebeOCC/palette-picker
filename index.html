How to use the wheel

  1. Drag the big dot to choose your main color. Near the edge = bold, near the middle = soft.
  2. Pick a combo. The small dots jump to colors that go with it.
  3. Choose Light or Dark. The page changes as you go.

Big dot = Primary · small dots = Secondary and Tertiary
